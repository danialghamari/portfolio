# Professional Work

| Project Link | Completion Date | Tools and Areas of Analysis | Project Summary | 
|---|---|---|---|
| [Efficient simulation of (quantum) chemical reactions](https://github.com/danialghamari/MASH-TPS/) | May 2026 | Python, PyTorch, HPC, Linux/Bash | Developed and implemented an end-to-end enhancing pipeline for efficient modeling of nonadiabtic transitions. The pipeline encompasses a simulation and a data analysis stages. The former integrates a mathematically rigorous approach (MASH) with the method of Transition Path Sampling, to efficiently generate a large statistical ensemble of reaction pathways. This combination demonstrated 10x speedup over alternative methods for the task (e.g plain MASH simulations). Then, in the latter stage, the pathways are analyzed in the feature space to extract various reaction descriptors. Furthermore, I investigated the Neural Network classifiers as a potential method to bypass the simulation stage. However, it did not prove sufficiently robust. This work culminated in an academic publication: [doi: 10.1063/5.0321299](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0321299) |
| [Simulation of biomolecular reactions with ML and quantum computer](https://github.com/danialghamari/gTPS) | Nov. 2023 | Python, Sklearn, Quantum Computing, HPC, Linux/Bash | Designed, engineered, and deployed a novel framework for the study of biological macromolecules reaction. This framework integrates an ML-enhanced Molecular Dynamics method, with mathematics of nuclear physics, and quantum computing to model reactions that would take specialized supercomputers (Anton) 3 months, in matter of days. The results have been published as articles in [Scientific Reports](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-20032-x) and [JCTC](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.3c01174) journals. |
